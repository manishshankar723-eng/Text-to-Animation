# The Last Bus — a short test script

**Characters**
- **Maya** — a woman in her late 20s, short curly black hair, yellow raincoat, carrying a backpack.
- **Sam** — a man in his 30s, tall and thin, grey hoodie, wireless headphones around his neck.

---

## Scene 1 — Bus Stop, Night

Rain falls on an empty city street. A single bus stop glows under a flickering streetlight.

MAYA hurries down the wet sidewalk, hood up, checking her phone. She stops at the bus stop, out of breath, and looks down the empty road. No bus.

SAM is already sitting on the bench, scrolling on his phone. He glances up at Maya, then back down.

MAYA: "Did the last bus already leave?"

SAM looks up, pulls the headphones off his neck. "Five minutes ago. We just missed it."

Maya sighs and sits on the far end of the bench. The rain gets heavier. Thunder rolls in the distance.

After a moment, Sam holds out half of his umbrella toward her. Maya smiles and slides closer.

Far down the street, two bright headlights appear through the rain — another bus, approaching.
